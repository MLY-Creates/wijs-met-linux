## 5. Install Linux 

If you are sure you want to put Linux on your computer, click the DVD icon in the top left corner labeled "Install Linux Mint". Then wait a moment for the installation window to appear.

> **Please note**
>
> Installing Linux will remove all files on your Windows computer. Make sure important photos, videos, and other files are saved elsewhere, such as a USB stick or the cloud.

The installation consists of 7 steps, let's go through them!

1. Select your language. On the left side of the panel, you will see a list of languages. Scroll down and click on Dutch. If the language is in Dutch, you can click continue.

2. Select your keyboard layout. This tells Linux how your keyboard is laid out. First, click "Dutch" on the left side. If your keyboard has a Windows key, choose "Dutch (US)" on the right. If your keyboard has a ⌘ symbol, choose "Dutch (Machintosh)" on the right. If that is successful, click continue.

3. Connect to your wifi (not required). Connect to the internet to immediately receive updates, automatically connect to printers, and be able to browse the internet once the installation is complete. Click on your home wifi network, and if a window opens, enter your wifi password. If you don't know it, it's often on the back of your router. If that is successful, click continue.

4. Multimedia support. To allow your computer to play many types of videos, and to load all websites properly, you need so-called "multimedia codecs". Check the box next to "Install multimedia codecs" and click continue.

5. Installation type. You can install Linux Mint on your computer in several ways, including "alongside" Windows. However, that often becomes a mess and is strongly discouraged. If you are installing Linux Mint on your computer, it is best to choose "erase disk and install Linux Mint".

6. Timezone. Here you choose your timezone, first click on Amsterdam in the map, and if that is also in the bar below the map, click continue.

7. Computer name. Here you choose the username for your computer. Here you choose the username for your computer, fill in the fields under name and username. You also choose your computer password here, make sure it is easy to remember and write it down in a password book.

If you click continue, you will receive another message asking if you are sure. If you have put all important files in another location. You can click "continue" and give your computer a new life.

After that, the installation will run automatically, make a coffee or tea and wait about 5 to 15 minutes, depending on the speed of your computer, until the installation is complete. Then click "restart" and remove the USB stick when asked.

Congratulations, the installation is now complete! After your computer has restarted, you can enjoy Linux. Want to know how? Go to the "Use Linux" section.
